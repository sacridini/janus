Abrupt change and disturbance

When and where the land changed suddenly: a clearing, a fire, a harvest, a thinning.

  • LandTrendr

    Year of detection, magnitude, duration and rate of the chosen loss or gain event.

    annual seriesGuide →

  • EWMACD

    Every observation against its training years through EWMA control charts: when it was disturbed, how far, when it recovered or gained, its state now.

    any dates, ≥ 2 per yearGuide →

  • BFAST

    Trend and season break counts, date and magnitude of the largest break.

    ≥ 2 dates per yearGuide →

  • BFAST Lite

    Break count, date and magnitude of the largest break, first break date.

    ≥ 2 dates per yearGuide →

  • BFAST Monitor

    First break in the monitoring period, its magnitude, break yes or no.

    ≥ 2 dates per yearGuide →

  • BEAST

    Trend and season averaged over thousands of models: the probability of a changepoint at each date, the most probable one with its jump, the mean number of changepoints.

    any series, ≥ 12 datesGuide →

  • CCDC

    Break count, largest break, NDVI change and segments from every reflectance band.

    one file per date, multibandGuide →

  • S-CCD

    CCDC's models carried forward by a Kalman filter: the breaks, and the state of every pixel at the last date (stable, change in progress and since when, waiting for a model).

    one file per date, multibandGuide →

  • CODED

    Forest degradation and deforestation from the NDFI of every observation, as strata and change dates.

    one file per date, multibandGuide →

Trends and recovery

Slow change over the years, and how fast a place grew back.

  • Recovery

    After LandTrendr's loss: years to recovery (Y2R), R80P, RRI and the absolute and year-on-year recovery.

    annual seriesGuide →

  • Mann-Kendall

    Significant Sen's slope, p-value and trend class, with Hamed-Rao, Yue-Wang and seasonal variants.

    any seriesGuide →

  • AR(1) trend

    The trend's t-test with autocorrelated errors (remotePARTS), and the regional Trend test by PARTS.

    any seriesGuide →

  • Linear trend of a fold

    A least-squares line over a statistic taken by period: the slope per period, R² and the t-test of the slope, as maps (FORCE's trend analysis).

    series with dates

  • CAT

    FORCE's change, aftereffect and trend: the greatest loss between the periods of a fold, and the linear trends of the whole fold, before the change and from it.

    series with dates

Seasons and phenology

When the growing season starts, peaks and ends, year by year.

  • Phenology

    Start, peak and end of season, length, amplitude, for a typical year or a chosen one.

    ≥ 6 dates per yearGuide →

  • Polar phenology

    FORCE's polar phenology: the start, middle and end of each season, the peak, the amplitudes, integrals and rates of rise and fall, 39 metrics by year. The series must be regular: run Interpolate first.

    regular series, ≥ 2 years

Cover and spectra

What a pixel is made of, from its spectrum, and the clouds that hide it.

  • NDFI

    Spectral unmixing of every date into a new layer of the NDFI, ready for the other tools.

    one file per date, multibandGuide →

  • Spectral unmixing

    The fraction of each cover (vegetation, soil, water...) whose spectrum you take from pins, at every date or in a hyperspectral scene.

    multiband or spectrumGuide →

  • Tmask

    The clouds and shadows the quality band missed, and the series without them as a new layer.

    green and SWIR1

Classes, clusters and segments

Maps of classes, from your samples or from the series alone, and the cleaning of them.

  • TWDTW

    A class map from patterns you take from pins on places you know.

    classification

  • Post-processing

    A classification cleaned: one-year flickers removed, a majority filter and a minimum mapping unit; models' probabilities smoothed by Bayesian smoothing (Camara et al. 2024).

    classes

  • SOM

    Clusters of alike trajectories, with the typical series of the one under the cursor.

    clustering

  • k-means

    Compact clusters of the whole series, named by the classes of your samples in them.

    clustering

  • SNIC

    Segments of alike series, their mean series as a layer and their polygons as a vector layer.

    segmentationGuide →

Spatial patterns

Where values cluster, where maps agree and how rough the land is.

  • Emerging hot spots

    Where values cluster high or low and how that changed: the Getis-Ord Gi* of every date and its trend, as ESRI's patterns (new, intensifying, persistent, sporadic... hot and cold spots).

    ≥ 3 datesGuide →

  • Agreement

    Where and when several change maps agree: the consensus year and how many back it.

    on result maps

  • Texture

    The grain of a landscape in a moving window: the GLCM's properties and window statistics of a temporal summary of the series, one map per feature, as inputs for a classifier.

    any series, whole-image runs only

  • EOF / principal components

    The few maps and time series that explain most of a window's variability, with each mode's share of the variance, North's test and an optional varimax rotation.

    ≥ 8 datesGuide →

  • Lagged correlation

    Where each pixel follows a driver (rainfall, a climate index, a regional mean) and after how many months: best r, best lag, an effective-n p-value and the pixels that pass the false discovery rate.

    ≥ 12 dates and a driverGuide →

Prepare the series

Smooth, fill, regularize and summarize a series before another method reads it.

  • Smoothing

    Whittaker and Savitzky-Golay, drawn over the series on the chart.

    chart only

  • CCDC synthetic image

    The image CCDC's models predict for one date: every band, free of clouds, gaps and noise.

    one file per date, multiband

  • Interpolate

    The series put on a regular grid of dates, every 16 days by default: an ensemble of radial basis functions, linear interpolation, a moving average or a robust harmonic model, as FORCE does.

    series with dates

  • Temporal metrics

    The 16 statistics of each pixel's whole series as maps: minimum, percentiles, median, maximum, range, mean, standard deviation, number of observations... (FORCE's STM).

    any series

  • Fold by period

    The same statistics by year, quarter, month, week or day of the year instead of over the whole series, one band per period.

    series with dates

  • Standardize

    Each pixel's series as z-scores or centred on its mean, missing observations left empty.

    any series

  • Clear-sky observations

    How many valid observations each pixel has in windows of months, and the gaps in days between them (FORCE's CSO).

    series with dates
