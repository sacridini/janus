Change detection · forest degradation

CODED and the NDFI, step by step.

Logging and understory fire leave a forest standing, and most indices barely see them. The NDFI does: it splits every pixel into green vegetation, dead wood, soil and shade. CODED models it over a training period and calls a change when it falls well below, then tells degradation (still forest after) from deforestation.

01The idea

Spectral mixture analysis reads each pixel's six reflectances as a mix of a few pure materials, the endmembers: green vegetation (GV), non-photosynthetic vegetation (NPV: dead wood, dry leaves), soil, shade and cloud. The NDFI (Normalized Difference Fraction Index, Souza et al. 2005) combines them:

NDFI = (GVshade − (NPV + soil)) / (GVshade + NPV + soil)

where GVshade is the green vegetation normalized by the shade. Closed forest is near 1; a canopy opened by logging or a fire drops, while it still reads as forest to the NDVI; pasture and bare soil go below 0.

CODED (Continuous Degradation Detection, Bullock et al. 2020) fits a harmonic model of the NDFI over a training period, then watches every later observation: a few in a row far below the model are a change. After it, a new model; if its mean NDFI is still that of a forest, the change was degradation, otherwise deforestation.

Both need Landsat-style surface reflectance (blue, green, red, NIR, SWIR1, SWIR2) with real dates; CODED also needs at least two observations a year, and a quality band helps both.

The screenshots, saved as PNGs in the folder shots:

janus --shots coded folder shots

The tour drives the guide's steps in a window of 1680 × 1050 and saves the window and its panels as PNGs in shots; the full-window ones are then converted to JPEG.

03The NDFI

Tools → Spectral analysis → NDFI (spectral unmixing) unmixes every observation with the endmembers of Souza et al. (2005). Run on the raster, it writes the NDFI of every date as a new series, opened as a layer over the others, ready for LandTrendr, BFAST or the agreement; and three maps: the mean NDFI, the lowest, and the observations used.

Max cloud fraction (0.05) leaves out the observations whose unmixed cloud fraction is larger: thin clouds and haze the quality band missed.

04CODED on the chart

Tools → Change detection → CODED (degradation). Its bands are found as for CCDC. On the chart, with the layer showing a normalized difference, CODED adds the NDFI of each observation, the training mean, and a vertical line at each change.

In Janus

  1. Open the same folder of Landsat dates (File → Open folder...), NIR as the band and red for the normalized difference.
  2. Drop a pin on the forest cleared in May–June 2022.
  3. Open Tools → Change detection → CODED (degradation), set Monitoring starts to 2022 and Training years to 2, and tick Fit the cursor, pins and ROI series.
  4. The chart draws the NDFI and the changes CODED finds on it.

In Python

"""Janus 1.17.0 (2026-11-07): pin 1 of landsat, reproduced outside Janus with Zeit.
Needs: pip install "zeit-cdts>=3.2.1"
"""
import zeit

# Pin 1: column 896, row 186 of the files, its centre at x -63.1191015, y -10.1301365 (EPSG:4326)
src = "landsat"
qa = zeit.load_raster(src, band=7, masked=True, pixel=(896, 186))  # QA_PIXEL
bands_cube = zeit.load_raster(
    src, bands={"blue": 1, "green": 2, "red": 3, "nir": 4, "swir1": 5, "swir2": 6}, scale=True,
    masked=True, pixel=(896, 186)
)
bands_cube = bands_cube.where(zeit.qa_to_fmask(qa, "landsat_c2") <= 1)  # only the clear observations (Fmask 0 and 1)
result = zeit.coded(bands_cube, start=2022.0, train_years=2.0)

print(result)

05The parameters

ParameterDefaultWhat it does
Reflectance unitsautoThe endmembers are reflectances: 0–1, × 10000 or Collection 2 digital numbers, detected from the values.
Monitoring start0The year monitoring starts; the training period is before it. 0: the training starts with the series. Here 2022.
Training period3Years of training, whose NDFI model the monitoring compares with. Here 2, all the series has before 2022.
Consecutive observations3Observations in a row beyond the threshold that make a change.
Threshold (RMSEs)3How far below the model, in RMSEs of the training fit.
Years after a change3Modelled as the land after a change, and the least time between two changes.
Min observations6Least observations to fit a model.
ChangeslossOnly drops of the NDFI, as CODED, or rises too.
Max changes3Changes kept per pixel.
Forest NDFI0.5A model with a mean NDFI at least this is forest: before the change (forest or not), after it (degradation or deforestation).
Max cloud fraction0.05Observations with more unmixed cloud are left out.

06The maps

Run over the visible area (14 s here; about 80 s for the whole 1.2 million pixels), CODED maps:

MapMeaning
StrataForest, non-forest, degradation, deforestation, or disturbance (a change outside forest).
First / last change dateThe earliest and the latest change.
NDFI change at the first changeThe NDFI after it minus before it.
ChangesHow many changes.
Mean NDFI of the training periodWhat the land was before.

07How strict

With only two years of training, about nine clear observations, the model of a forest pixel is tight, and ordinary variation from one dry season to the next crosses 3 RMSEs: above, much of the standing forest reads as degraded. At 5 RMSEs only the strong drops are left: the clearings, in red, and far less orange. There is no right threshold in general. Check it on pins over forest you know untouched and over logging you know of, and give CODED a longer training period when the series allows.

08What next

  • Every band, every break: CCDC on the same series.
