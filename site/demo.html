Try it: forty years of one forest frontier.

This is a window of 256 × 256 Landsat pixels in Rondônia, Brazil, with one NDVI value per pixel for each year from 1985 to 2024. Move the pointer over the map to see the past of the pixel under it. The straight segments on the chart are LandTrendr's fit.

In Janus

The same gesture works on whole scenes of any size: you hover a pixel on the map and its series appears on the chart. You tune the fit on the chart, on one pixel at a time, and then run it on the whole image.

The LandTrendr guide goes through that on a full image, and Methods lists the others.

Download Janus LandTrendr guide All methods

Built in Rust with egui, wgpu and GDAL. Direct3D 12 on Windows, Vulkan on Linux, Metal on macOS.
